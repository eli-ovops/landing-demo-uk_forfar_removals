# Current UK reference set — principles, not copying

Accessed 2026-08-29. These are public reference sites for information and conversion patterns. No code, copy, imagery, trademarks, layouts, or brand assets may be copied.

| Reference | What was observed publicly | Transferable principle only |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Company Moves & Storage](https://www.companymoves.co.uk/) | Clear domestic/commercial/storage categorisation and quote-led entry. | Let customers self-identify their move type before asking them to absorb a full service catalogue. |
| [Blue Sky Removals](https://www.blueskyremovals.co.uk/) | Removals/storage positioning with distinct quote routes. | Separate service decision and enquiry action; do not hide storage behind a long list. |
| [City Moves](https://citymoves.co.uk/) | A removals/storage proposition with an explicit quote cue. | A single decisive next step can co-exist with concise service range. |
| [Get Moved](https://www.get-moved.co.uk/) | Family-run removals positioning, service-area framing and secure storage in the public proposition. | Local/family credibility works best when tied to concrete services/areas, not generic superlatives. |
| [Hampson & Mealey](https://hampsonmealey.co.uk/get-a-quote/) | Public quote page separates a removal quote from storage availability and lists contact routes. | Give customers a legible route by need; do not imitate its form or operational claims. |

## Design read for a future authorised direction

Forfar’s own asset strength is the branded lorry and burgundy identity; the opportunity is not to mimic a glossy national mover. The reference principles favour: a quick local-service identification, clear household/commercial/storage selection, proof before anxiety, and one low-friction contact path. Any actual Demo must independently satisfy the project’s pre-code, rights, client-visible text, and desktop/mobile gates.
