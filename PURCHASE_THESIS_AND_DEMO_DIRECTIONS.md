# £600 purchase thesis and two research-only Demo directions

Status: recommendations only. No HTML/CSS/JS, image generation, asset download, Demo, mock-up, deployment, or customer-visible material was created.

## £600 Purchase Thesis

1. **Five-second recognition:** a future authorised first screen can say “Removals & Storage across Angus, Tayside & Fife” and show an approved real branded vehicle/Forfar identity; visitors should recognise the business before reading a paragraph.
2. **Customer specificity:** use only verified facts: Forfar identity, named service range, local coverage, generic contact route, and carefully attributed public proof. No invented crew, jobs, pricing, dates, review claims, membership status or availability.
3. **Commercial delta from current site:** transform the existing generic “look around” opening and dense service nav into a short decision route: household move / business move / storage, backed by care/reliability proof and a visible quote path. The intended delta is clarity and confidence, not a promise of more enquiries.
4. **Hero to bottom-40% story:** proposition + primary contact → service choice → proof/why choose Forfar → packing/storage reassurance → final quote/contact block. This helps a prospective mover progress from “what do they do?” to “can they handle my move?” to “how do I ask?” without pretending to offer instant booking.

## Direction A — “Move day, made clear”

| Element | Recommendation |
| --- | --- |
| Intent | Calm, local, trustworthy household-moving route built from the real vehicle/Forfar mark, not stock-house imagery. |
| Hero | Approved branded lorry image; concise “Your Move, Your Way” supporting line; coverage and one “Request a quote” action. |
| Service slice | Domestic removals, packing/unpacking, short/long-term storage. Keep commercial/self-storage as clear secondary links or scope, not invented pages. |
| Proof | Attributable review count/rating only if live/current verification and permission are completed; otherwise use verified family-run/40+ years claims only with later confirmation. |
| Bottom 40% | A packing/storage reassurance block, service-area line, then phone/email/approved quote action. No fake enquiry form. |
| Mobile | One-column header, a full-width contact/quote action, 42–60px touch controls, no horizontal overflow, proof before secondary nav. |
| Motion | Intensity 1/5: no autoplay; optional subtle reveal only after future motion-skill and performance/accessibility review. |
| Main risk | Asset/review/membership rights are unverified. |

## Direction B — “One local team, three practical routes”

| Element | Recommendation |
| --- | --- |
| Intent | More operational and choice-led: orient household, commercial and storage visitors in one glance. |
| Hero | Forfar mark + local coverage; three large service-route panels using only approved own assets/abstract colour blocks. |
| Service slice | Home removals; commercial moves/archive storage; self-storage/packaging. Avoid making external/real-time storage availability claims. |
| Proof | A compact “careful planning / packing / storage” sequence grounded in listed services, not fabricated process guarantees. |
| Bottom 40% | “What do you need help with?” recap, map/coverage only if permission/accuracy is cleared, primary phone/email/approved quote CTA. |
| Mobile | Stack each route as a clearly labelled card; fixed header CTA; keep contact information completely inside 390px. |
| Motion | Intensity 2/5: optional card focus transition only; no background video or automated slider. |
| Main risk | Could imply a complex branching quote tool if overbuilt; it must remain static presentation and contact routing. |

Direction A is preferred: it makes better use of the distinctive fleet imagery and directly counters the current generic hero. Direction B is a credible alternative when service routing proves more commercially important after client confirmation.
